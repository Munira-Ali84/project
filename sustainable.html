PRACTICAL GUIDELINES

6 Practical Sustainable Tips

💧 Water Conservation

Reduce daily shower times and fix leaking campus or household faucets immediately.

☀️ Energy Saving

Turn off unused classroom lights, air conditioners, and unplug electronic equipment.

♻️ Recycling

Separate paper, plastic, and electronic waste using designated campus recycling bins.

🦅 Wildlife Protection

Respect natural habitats and refrain from disturbing Saudi Arabia's indigenous species.

🌳 Tree Planting

Participate in campus greening initiatives aligning with the 10 Billion Trees target.

⚡ Renewable Energy

Learn about solar energy projects and adopt energy-efficient LED technology.
